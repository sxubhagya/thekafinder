Theka Finder FAQ

Answering life's most pressing questions. Dialect tuned to local frequencies. 🍻

🤔 What is a "Theka"?

Listen up, rookie. A Theka (pronounced thay-kaa) is a government-licensed retail shop in India where spirits are sold. Forget cozy aisles and wine-tasting notes. In a real theka, speed is everything. You slide your money through a tiny metal grille, scream your order, get a bottle wrapped in a black polythene bag, and make way for the next guy. Simple, efficient, beautiful.

🧭 What does the compass do?

It is a literal Daaru Direction System (DDS). Instead of typing "liquor shop near me" on maps and scrolling through outdated listings, this compass points a giant beer bottle straight at the nearest checked theka. Spin around—the needle tracks in real-time. Just follow the bottle!

🏃 What is "Steps to Beer"?

Distance in meters is fine, but how many physical steps stand between you and your cold beverage? We calculate this using an average human stride (~0.76 meters). Think of it as your cardio motivation index.

🚖 What's the Uber button for?

Drinking and driving is an absolute NO-GO. Absolutely no car-o-bar (tailgate drinking) if you're behind the wheel. If the nearest theka is too far to walk, hit the "Book Cab" button to load Uber with your target theka's coordinates pre-filled. Arrive like a VIP, return safely.

❌ Why is the compass spinning like crazy?

Ah, you've hit a Dry Zone. If you are in Gujarat, Bihar, Nagaland, Mizoram, or Lakshadweep (or if it's a national Dry Day), legal thekas are non-existent. The compass needle spins indefinitely in grief. Put the glass down and hydrate, boss.

👍/👎 Why report Open/Closed?

In India, theka timings are unpredictable. They might shut down for a local election, a festival, or because the owner wanted to go home early. The crowdsourced voting system calculates a real-time consensus so you don't walk all the way just to find a locked shutter. Help the gang out—cast your vote!

🚨 What is the SOS button?

The Emergency Beer SOS. If you are stranded or desperately need your friends to bring backup, hitting the SOS button opens WhatsApp with a prefilled, witty message containing your exact GPS coordinates and a search link. Help is on the way!
