Guide

Answering life's most pressing questions.

What is a "theka"?

Listen up, rookie. A Theka (pronounced thay-kaa) is a government-licensed retail shop in India where spirits are sold. Forget cozy aisles and wine-tasting notes. In a real theka, speed is everything. You slide your money through a tiny metal grille, scream your order, get a bottle wrapped in a black polythene bag, and make way for the next guy. Simple, efficient, beautiful.

What does the compass do?

It is a literal Daaru Direction System (DDS). Instead of typing "liquor shop near me" on maps and scrolling through outdated listings, this compass points a giant beer bottle straight at the nearest theka. Spin around and the bottle tracks in real time. Just follow the bottle!

What are "steps to beer"?

Distance in meters is fine, but how many physical steps stand between you and your cold beverage? We calculate this using an average human stride (~0.76 meters). Think of it as your cardio motivation index.

What's the cab button for?

Drinking and driving is an absolute NO-GO. Absolutely no car-o-bar (tailgate drinking) if you're behind the wheel. If the nearest theka is too far to walk, hit Cab to open Uber with your pickup set to where you are and the theka pre-filled as the destination. Arrive like a VIP, return safely.

Why is the compass spinning?

Ah, you've hit a Dry Zone. Gujarat, Bihar, Nagaland, Mizoram and Lakshadweep have no legal thekas, so there the bottle points at the nearest wet town (a "border oasis") instead. If the dial itself spins in grief, there is simply nothing to point at: no shop within 15 km. Put the glass down and hydrate, boss.

Why report open or closed?

In India, theka timings are unpredictable. They might shut for a local election, a festival, or because the owner wanted to go home early. Opening hours come live from Google, but when enough people say the opposite (five or more reports in 12 hours, and most of them disagree), their word wins. Help the gang out and cast your vote!

What does SOS do?

The Emergency Beer SOS. If you are stranded or desperately need your friends to bring backup, hitting SOS opens WhatsApp with a prefilled, witty message containing your exact GPS coordinates and a search link. Help is on the way!
